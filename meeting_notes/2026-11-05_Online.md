## Topics discussed

- RSECon27
  - The conference committee has been formed from 54 applications and onboarding is underway.
  - The conference web team is working towards an initial website covering the venue, dates and conference vision.
- Finance
  - Work continues to finalise the previous year's accounts, have them signed off and submit the Charity Commission return.
  - Discussed potential uses for accumulated Society funds, including increasing the Events and Initiatives fund, supporting SocRSE Fellows, and activities around 15 years of RSE.
- Discussed the proposed "What next for the RSE movement in the UK?" event series. Questions were raised around trustee participation, support for participant travel, potential conflicts of interest, hybrid delivery and opportunities to combine events with existing regional meetings. The proposal will return for a vote after these questions have been addressed.
- Discussed plans to mark 15 years of RSE. Trustees were asked to contribute ideas, with the communications team planning to begin activity in January.
- The LinkedIn Community Hub has been established as a distinct group, separate from the main RSE LinkedIn group.
- Discussed rebooting the Society Advisory Board, including its purpose, membership, size and meeting frequency, and how to include a wider range of perspectives without creating unnecessary administrative overhead.
- Discussed the Society's membership of the National Council for Voluntary Organisations (NCVO) and whether the Society is making sufficient use of the membership.

## Topics discussed

- New trustee roles assigned.
- Finance
  - An updated monthly finance report was not available for the meeting and will be produced before the next meeting.
  - Post-year-end adjustments to the 2025/26 accounts are still being processed. Updated final accounts are targeted for approval at the November trustee meeting, followed by completion of the independent examination and annual Charity Commission return.
- Discussed options for in-person trustee meeting for coming year, still looking for hosts.
- Discussed proposals from the RSECon27 Programme Chairs to add a Sustainability Chair and a "Conference Experience and Impact Team" to the conference steering group. It was also suggested that the EDIA Chair should be represented on the steering committee.
- Discussed whether the current quorum requirement of one third of trustees for a binding vote is too low.

## Topics discussed

- RSECon26
  - 317 in-person and 24 online registrations as of 5 August.
  - Sponsorship payments and bursary advances are being processed; some sponsorship payments may arrive after the deadline and unconfirmed bursaries may be reallocated.
  - Current budget scenarios continue to predict a surplus, although the estimated cost of providing screens to sponsors needs adjusting.
  - £10,000 remains set aside for unplanned conference expenditure and is currently unused.
- End-of-year accounts are being finalised, with the aim of completing the independent examination in time for the AGM. If this is not possible, the best available management accounts will accompany the AGM notification and annual report.
- Membership renewals were discussed. The number overdue is slightly higher than usual, although some members listed as overdue have active direct debits pending.
- Election voting tokens were discussed following two tokens being sent to some members. Members will be informed that both are valid.
- AGM 2026 planning
  - Discussed whether former trustees could support the onboarding of new trustees during a handover period.
  - Discussed delegation of powers to non-trustees and questions around trustee numbers and terms.
  - Groups will review and update their content for the annual report.
- Proposed RSECon26 Society contribution workshop (P&F WG), including an international panel and broader discussion of issues affecting RSE. The session should avoid focusing exclusively on AI.
- Discussed updating SIG Terms of Reference around training requirements and whether a central training list would be useful across multiple groups.
- RSECon26 Code of Conduct arrangements, including ensuring the website reflects policy and identifying CoC members with lanyards at the conference.
- Agreed to order a replacement SocRSE banner.
- Discussed the possibility of a SocRSE Fellowship programme in light of there being no SSI Fellowship in 2028.
